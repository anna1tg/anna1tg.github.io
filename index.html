About me

I'm Anna Aleshko, an AI-powered creative director from Kyiv. I direct generative tools the way a film director works with a crew, so the work looks made by hand.

AI can make a thousand images in an hour. Knowing which one is right for your brand is still a human job, and that is my job. Every project starts with a question, not a prompt: who is this for, and what should they feel?

From there I write the brief, direct the generations, refine light, mood and detail, and edit until every frame of a campaign looks like it belongs to the same world. I also design and style interiors, kids' rooms most of all, so I care about how a space feels as much as how it looks.
